Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tarot Reader AI (the “App”) handles information when you use the service.

Information processed

The App may process an installation identifier, language and app settings, purchase and subscription status, usage events needed to provide readings, the questions you submit, generated reading content, and technical diagnostics such as error details. We do not require your real name, phone number, or social-network account to use the App.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver AI-assisted tarot readings, maintain reading credits and purchase entitlements, restore subscriptions, prevent duplicate transactions and abuse, provide support, diagnose failures, and improve reliability.

AI processing and service providers

Your question may be sent to contracted AI and infrastructure providers solely to generate the requested reading and operate the service. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple’s own privacy terms. Providers receive only the information necessary to perform their services.

Storage and retention

Chat history is primarily stored locally on your device. Server-side transaction records, entitlement data, limited request metadata, and operational logs may be retained for security, accounting, support, and legal obligations. Temporary generation data is deleted when it is no longer required for processing or troubleshooting.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored history by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it. You may request information about, correction of, or deletion of server-side data linked to your user identifier by contacting support. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Tarot content is provided for entertainment and reflection, not as professional advice.

Security and changes

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when the App or legal requirements change; the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the contact options on the Support page.
